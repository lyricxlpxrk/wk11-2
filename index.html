STEP 6 · HERE AND NOW

Past or now?

You already know the past tense. Now, look at the picture again and describe what is happening here and now!

YESTERDAY · PAST

A boy played soccer in the park.

A girl read a book.

RIGHT NOW · PRESENT

A boy is playing soccer in the park.

A girl is reading a book.

Think about the difference.
Past: 동사의 과거형을 사용해요.
Now: am / is / are + 동사-ing를 사용해요.

Mission 1. Describe what is happening now.

Write 3–5 sentences. You can look at the picture while writing.

Mission 2. Change past into now!

Change each past-tense sentence into a sentence about what is happening now.

1. A boy played soccer in the park.

2. A girl read a book on the bench.

1. A boy is playing soccer in the park.
2. A girl is reading a book on the bench.

Great work!

You described a picture using the present tense and compared it with the past tense. You can now describe what happened and what is happening!
